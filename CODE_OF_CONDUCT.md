# Code of conduct

Juno welcomes contributors regardless of identity, background, technical experience, or role. Treat people with respect, give actionable feedback, honor privacy, and make space for disagreement about the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.

This code applies in project discussions, reviews, issues, events, and other spaces where participants represent Juno. Report conduct privately to a maintainer through an existing trusted channel. If the concern involves that maintainer, contact an uninvolved maintainer when one is available. A confidential public-facing reporting route and escalation contact must be established before public launch.

An uninvolved maintainer should acknowledge the report, limit information to those handling it, document the decision privately, and apply a proportionate response. Responses may include a warning, removal of content, or temporary or permanent exclusion. Reporters and participants must be protected from retaliation. An uninvolved maintainer may reconsider a decision if new information is provided.
