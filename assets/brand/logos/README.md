Light and dark variants include their intended backdrop. White and black variants are transparent. Place white on a dark surface and black on a light surface. The kit was supplied by the project creator. Preserve the master geometry, creator and contributor attribution, and relevant notices. Use [derived icons](../icons/README.md) for small browser or application surfaces and follow the [usage guide](../guidelines/README.md).

The supplied standalone symbols use square 128-unit canvases. PNG versions are available for [light](juno-symbol-light.png), [dark](juno-symbol-dark.png), [white](juno-symbol-white.png), and [black](juno-symbol-black.png). These are the current supplied variants. Preserve matching SVG and PDF files.
