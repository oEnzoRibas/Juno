## Context and problem

Describe the decision, affected modules, and why it is needed now.

## Drivers and requirements

List constraints, priorities and measurable evaluation requirements.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Compare credible options against the drivers, including delaying adoption if viable.

## Selected solution and rationale

For a proposal, record the recommendation and open questions. For an accepted decision, record the selection, rationale, approver and acceptance date.

## Consequences

State benefits, drawbacks, security and privacy implications, operational impact, migration or rollback requirements and any deferred work.

## Validation strategy and evidence

List acceptance evidence, exact completed checks, and gaps. Include security, tenancy and recovery checks where relevant.

## References

Link primary sources and related specifications.
