# Membership lifecycle decisions for maintainer approval

Status: proposed. These options define future writes under [JUNO-0028](../development/tasks/JUNO-0028.md). The existing read-only directory and restricted profile editor can continue independently.

| Decision                  | Recommended option                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Alternatives and security impact                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reactivation              | An active administrator of the same organization may reactivate an existing membership through an explicit, audited action. Restore ordinary access initially. Require a separate approved grant to restore elevated authority. Reevaluate the old organization's permissions immediately on deactivation. Preserve unrelated tenant memberships. | Restoring the prior role is simpler but can revive stale administrative privilege. A new invitation establishes fresh consent but requires a defined way to reuse the existing account and preserve the original membership record.                                                      |
| Last active administrator | Reject deactivation, downgrade or self-removal if it would leave zero active administrators. Serialize the invariant within the tenant in the same database transaction and require successful concurrent negative tests. Escalate exceptional recovery through a separately approved operator process.                                           | A UI-only or pre-write count permits concurrent requests to leave an orphan tenant. A platform override can recover abandoned tenants but increases cross-tenant authority and needs narrow, audited approval.                                                                           |
| Delegation                | Keep platform, organization, and future directorate, team and project grants explicit and scoped. Avoid transitive grant rights by default. Require grant and revoke audit events. Confirm who may appoint another organization administrator before implementation.                                                                              | Tenant-wide manager roles are easier to administer but increase the impact of a compromised account. Delegated grants require explicit scope, expiry or revoke behavior, and negative tests for self-escalation and foreign resources.                                                   |
| History retention         | Preserve attributable membership transitions and historical allocations after deactivation. Restrict access to responsible tenant actors. Record event and actor identifiers with minimum metadata. Set a retention period, export behavior and anonymization process by maintainer decision before real member data.                             | Indefinite retention improves historical reporting but accumulates personal data. Early deletion reduces exposure but weakens reproducibility and auditability. Anonymization can preserve reporting while limiting exposure when the retention target and audit requirements permit it. |

Acceptance requires tenant-scoped write tests, attempts by members and platform-only users, concurrent last-administrator operations, reactivation without restored privileges, and audit integrity and retention behavior. Until a decision is recorded, these options carry no runtime authority.
