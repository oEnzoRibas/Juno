# AI-assisted development

Juno was created by Enzo Ribas and is developed with the assistance of artificial intelligence.

AI tools may help plan the product, compare architectures, generate or revise code, draft documentation, propose tests, review changes, research technical options, and explore design and accessibility decisions. Disclose meaningful AI assistance in pull requests, including affected areas and verification performed.

Generated material can contain incorrect assumptions, invented APIs or results, security defects, inaccessible interactions, and incompatible or unattributed third-party material. Verify output against requirements and authoritative sources, run relevant checks, and confirm the provenance of reused material. Keep secrets, private member information, and confidential third-party content out of AI inputs. Preserve contributor credit and notices. AI tools receive no authorship or copyright attribution.

Human maintainers are responsible for reviewing, validating, and accepting contributions. Describe what review and testing actually occurred. Record any remaining human review. See [contribution guidance](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [governance](GOVERNANCE.md).
