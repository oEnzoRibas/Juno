# Juno brand usage proposal

**Status:** Draft for human review. This document makes no claim of trademark registration.

The Juno name, `juno.` wordmark, monogram, and official visual identity identify the Juno project. The [MIT License](LICENSE) governs use, modification, and distribution of the software and associated project documentation. This proposal addresses representations of affiliation.

## Proposed guidance

- Use “Juno” to accurately identify the software, cite releases, report compatibility, or acknowledge its origin. Preserve license notices and contributor credit where applicable.
- Give independently maintained forks or hosted instances a distinct visual identity where practical. Make their operator and support contact clear. Example wording is “Independent deployment using Juno, operated by Example Junior Enterprise.”
- Use official release, partnership, and endorsement claims only with project maintainer approval. Follow the [brand guidelines](assets/brand/guidelines/README.md) when presenting authorized official materials.
- Tenant logos and other organizational assets are supplied by their respective organizations. Operators should check applicable permissions.

## Asset scope and adoption

The repository inventories local artwork references in the [brand asset guide](assets/brand/README.md). Separate brand-asset terms are pending review. Before distributing a separate brand asset package, confirm ownership, master files, applicable notices, covered paths, and rights granted. Publish any approved asset terms with the files and preserve MIT software permissions.

The project owner should review the proposed naming guidance, examples, asset scope, and permission contact before adoption.
