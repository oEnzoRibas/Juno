# character design

Read the construction and expression plan in `docs/design/mascot/README.md`. Overlay eye line, ear roots, muzzle length and shoulder height across views. Turn the broad muzzle in perspective. Keep the scarf attachment and pale tail tip consistent across mirrored views. For a new emotion, test brow, eye and muzzle separately while holding head geometry constant. Check the outline with the interior hidden. Present exploratory deviations for review before making them canonical.
