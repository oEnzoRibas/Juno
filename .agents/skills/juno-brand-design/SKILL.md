# juno brand design

Read `docs/design/brand-identity.md`, `assets/brand/README.md` and `docs/design/mascot/README.md`. Preserve the wordmark and J monogram. The mascot is Juno, a young wolf. Match the maintainer-supplied eight-expression sheet, especially its cheek tufts, amber eyes and golden scarf. Anchor new work to the selected teammate gesture and explain what is deliberately varied. Record source, method, selection and approval separately. Seek usage approval before placing art on a product screen. Keep essential instructions in accessible text.
