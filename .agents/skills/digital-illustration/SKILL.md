# digital illustration

Use the full-body reference and editable head studies inventoried in `docs/design/mascot/README.md`. Block the silhouette, then separate lit fur, ruff, cream muzzle and shadow. Prioritize a readable gesture before adding texture. Preserve native shape masks and separate eyes, highlights and scarf. Keep a hidden reference and independent backgrounds. Proof the sRGB export against light and dark surfaces at its intended size, inspect the alpha edge, and include editable source with the handoff.
